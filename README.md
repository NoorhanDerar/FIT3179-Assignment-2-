About the Project

This project explores restaurant ratings in Malaysia using 361,784 Google and TripAdvisor reviews across ten cities. It investigates what star ratings actually tell us about restaurants and why ratings can differ between platforms, cities, and types of restaurants.

The visualisation explores several questions:

Review coverage: Which Malaysian cities are represented in the data, and which are missing?

Star ratings: How often do restaurants receive high or low ratings?

Platform differences: Do Google and TripAdvisor rate the same cities differently?

Rating distributions: Can restaurants with similar average ratings have very different review patterns?

Cuisine: Are some cuisine types rated higher than others?

Review content: What words appear in happy and unhappy reviews?

Household income: Is state income related to restaurant ratings?

Changes over time: How have TripAdvisor ratings and city rankings changed from 2015 to 2020?

The project presents these findings as a scrolling visual story, using charts and maps to help readers compare patterns and understand the limitations of star ratings.
